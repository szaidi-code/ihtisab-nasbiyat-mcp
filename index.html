Available MCP Tools

  • list_volumes — Lists all 12 volumes, total pages, book counts, and chapter counts.
  • list_books — Lists all 54 books with English & Urdu titles, authors, page boundaries, and scholarly summaries.
  • get_book_details — Retrieves full metadata, English summary, and complete English & Urdu Table of Contents for any book (1–54).
  • search_catalog — Full-text SQLite FTS5 search in English, Urdu, or transliterated terms across all 54 books, 2,277 chapter headings, and translated pages.
  • extract_pdf_pages — Renders 1–10 pages from a volume and returns cached English translations.
  • save_page_translation — Indexes full-page English & Urdu translations into the SQLite FTS5 database.
